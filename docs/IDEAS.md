# Ideas to try

Things to try that come from the Sly 1 decomp's compiler notes and our own findings, written for our near misses. Each is a hypothesis, not a
result: measure it (`scripts/matching/try.py`, `classify_diffs.py`, `regalloc.py`, `sched1.py`, `globalalloc.py`), then tick it, strike it, or
move what you learned into [contributing/lessons.md](contributing/lessons.md). Mechanisms are in [toolchain/](toolchain/README.md); rules that limit
a lever are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). Format: `- [ ] **Title**: what to try; which near miss it fits; how to measure`. Add the date and the
result when you close one. A zero from a search tool is a lead, not a match: read the diff (a search once kept a `commute` edit that changed the
polynomial's meaning).
